# Wheel App

An application for Android better than the popular wheeling app.

Wheel App is a native Android decision-maker built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Spin a fully customizable wheel to randomly pick a winner from your own list of options, or flip a coin for quick yes/no decisions — all with a clean, modern interface.

Most "wheel picker" tools out there live in a browser tab — slow to load, full of ads, and useless without an internet connection. Wheel App is different: it's a real Android app that lives on your phone, opens instantly, works completely offline, and keeps every wheel you build saved locally so you never have to type your list out again. Whether you're picking a restaurant with friends, running a giveaway, assigning chores, deciding who goes first in a game, or just flipping a coin to settle an argument, Wheel App handles it faster and cleaner than doing it on a website.

The whole app is built from the ground up with modern Android tooling — Kotlin, Jetpack Compose for the UI, and a local Room database for storage — so it's lightweight, responsive, and doesn't rely on shady third-party trackers or ad networks to function.

## Features

- **Custom Spinning Wheel** — Add, edit, remove, and color your own wheel slices to build any list you want (raffles, decisions, games, giveaways, etc.)
- **Coin Flip** — A dedicated animated coin flip screen for quick heads-or-tails decisions
- **Save & Favorite Wheels** — Save your wheel configurations and mark favorites for quick access later
- **Spin History** — Automatically tracks past spin results so you can look back at previous winners
- **Sound Effects** — Toggleable sound on spin completion and coin flips
- **Themes** — Switch between light/dark app themes
- **Multi-language Support** — Built-in translation system for multiple languages
- **User Profiles** — Sign in (including anonymous login) to sync your wheels and history
- **Smooth Animations** — Fluid wheel spin and coin flip animations built entirely in Jetpack Compose

## How It Works

1. Open the app and land on your wheel — add slice names and pick a color for each one.
2. Tap spin and watch the wheel animate to a random result, with a sound effect and popup showing the winner.
3. Save the wheel so it's ready next time, or mark it as a favorite for quick access.
4. Need a simpler decision? Switch to the Coin Flip tab for an animated heads-or-tails flip.
5. Check the History tab anytime to see what your past spins landed on.
